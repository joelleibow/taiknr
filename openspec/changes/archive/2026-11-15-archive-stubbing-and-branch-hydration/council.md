## Question and Scope

**The design question.** How does taiknr implement the archive half of its own premise — stage a large persisted tool result at its turn boundary, replace it at settlement with a one-line stub while keeping its non-text blocks, record the batch in the session, and rebuild the in-memory archive from the active branch — inside seven already-registered lifecycle events, without changing a locked handler signature, adding a production file, adding a dependency, or emitting a line of console output?

The change is not greenfield on a blank page: `src/domain/` and `src/use-cases/` already declare the shapes and the three seams, and `src/infra/adapters/pi-archive-adapter.ts` already owns `InMemoryArchiveStore` and `TAIKNR_SIDE_STORE_TYPE` (`council-codebase` checked every current-state claim against `HEAD`; CB-4, CB-5). What is missing is every body: three adapters, one domain function and three use cases throw `Not implemented`, and six of the seven handlers register and return (`Eleanor` §1; `council-codebase`'s opening verification). So the council's question is placement and edge behaviour, not invention.

**Constraints that bound every answer.** Joel's Q1 → (a) (carry staged items forward to the next settled run) and Q2 → (a) (hydrate the whole branch, so pre-compaction outputs stay recallable) are ruled and not reopened. D3–D5 fix inertness at the composition-root gate. D6 keeps `domain/` and `use-cases/` free of `@earendil-works/pi-*`. D7 locks the seven registrations and the adapter, port and registrar signatures against `HEAD` (`extension-packaging` "Signatures unchanged"). D8 keeps the tooling files and the committed lockfile. D10 puts classifier scoring, the `/taiknr` command, archive caps and pruning out of scope. S1–S6 settle the item id, the stub template, non-text preservation, the three hydration events, inertness and the docs pass. The two spec deltas under `specs/archive-stubbing/` and `specs/branch-hydration/` state the required behaviour; `Modified Capabilities` is `None`.

**Evidence the council worked from.** `proposal.md` and both spec deltas; `research-chidi.md` (documentation, Pi 1.5.2 source, persistence and compaction semantics) and `research-eleanor.md` (the codebase at `main` `4e951a2`); `michael-orientation.md`, including its ten traps and six open questions O1–O6; `owner-rulings.md`, carrying Joel's request, the standing rulings that bind, and his Q1 and Q2 answers. Seven lens reports were convened in parallel over that set: none edited a file or ran a check. Six open questions were put to the council; all six are now settled by the rulings below — O1 and O2 by M9 and M8, O3 by M1 and M5, O4 by M7, O5 by M10, O6 by M11.

## Lens Findings & Trade-offs

**Necessity — `council-zoom-out` (no findings).** Nothing here is a neighboring feature dressed as core work: the proposal implements the behaviour the package exists for, using seams that already exist. Its constraints are the useful part: no parallel archive service, no generic event framework, no validator dependency, no logging facility, no persistence abstraction, and no new production file. Its two open disagreements with other lenses — a small adapter helper plus a narrow `extension-packaging` delta, and a session harness that avoids `@earendil-works/pi-ai` — were both resolved against it (M7, M10).

**Contradiction hunting — `council-critic` (4 major, 9 minor).** The four majors are the sharp edges. `CR-1` argues that "committed" is in-memory while Pi decides afterwards whether drafts persist, so another extension can discard taiknr's whole boundary after taiknr has already recorded success — Q1 → (a)'s promise then quietly fails. `CR-2` shows the spec's "Pi's recovery omission" wording cannot be checked at all, because `ContextEditEntry` carries no author (`Chidi` §3), and that a non-null edit by another extension is overwritten as readily as a null one. `CR-3` finds the 180- and 120-character cuts can split a surrogate pair in a stub that is then permanently on the branch. `CR-4` argues only a real `AgentSession` can test the abort → `/tree` → settle path the change is built around. The nine minors are cost statements, single-home rules and observability recipes (`CR-7` duplication, `CR-8` unbounded maps, `CR-5`/`CR-9` seating an eligibility rule and the id pairing, `CR-10`–`CR-13` persisted-format fragility, silent drops and a threshold that can make stubs longer than the text they replace).

**UX and explanation — `council-comprehension` (4 major, 3 minor).** Two majors duplicate the critic's (`C-1` on an earlier extension's non-null replacement being defeated by a later stub, `C-2` on what "committed" can honestly mean when persistence is not transactional). The other two are its own and both bit: `C-4` shows that joining two empty text blocks with `"\n"` yields a one-character string that stages an empty stub at `minCharsToArchive: 0`, and `C-3` raises that a user will read stubbing as redaction or deletion when the raw text remains in the session JSONL, the exports and the side-store record. Its minors ask for a deterministic duplicate-id winner (`C-5`), for carry-forward's runtime bound to be stated (`C-6`), and for the re-enable and silent-skip behaviours to be explained (`C-7`).

**Structure — `council-structural` (4 major, 5 minor).** The majors are all "one fact, one home": the persisted item shape is depended on by both deltas and defined by neither (`CS-1`), the per-id auxiliary maps have entry and exit but no release rule (`CS-2`), the drop filter encodes a mechanism where it should encode an invariant (`CS-3`), and `pi-archive-adapter.ts` becomes the archive half's god module after this change (`CS-4`). `CS-4` also carries the council's only structural *proposal*: move the Pi-free `InMemoryArchiveStore` and `TAIKNR_SIDE_STORE_TYPE` to their own file. It was set aside (M7). Its five minors are the handler-census drift (`CS-5`), the restated default threshold (`CS-6`), the untransactional-commit position (`CS-7`), the duplicate winner (`CS-8`) and eligibility across two layers (`CS-9`).

**Readiness — `council-readiness` (6 major, 6 minor, 2 owner questions).** The sharpest lens on blockers Derek would otherwise invent. `R-1` finds O1 unanswerable inside the locked types: `turn-end.ts` receives `argsByCallId` as a `ReadonlyMap` and `agent-before-settle.ts` does not receive it at all, so there is no legal place to discard entries. `R-2` is the ordering hazard: the store is mutated at handler time, Pi validates the whole draft list afterwards, and a partial persist with the `context_edit` drafts landing before the side-store draft leaves stubs whose raw text is unrecoverable after a resume. `R-3` is the one-way door: a bare, unversioned array of unenumerated items freezes a persisted format. `R-4`, `R-5` and `R-6` are the missing homes — the branch data source, the image-only guard, and result-to-entry pairing. Its two owner questions (versioned envelope; PoC-era sessions) are settled by M1 and M2, not by Joel.

**Codebase — `council-codebase` (5 minor, 0 major).** Every current-state claim the proposal and research make checks out on disk, and the Impact section is a clean boundary with nothing missing and nothing extra. Its value is three constraints nobody else found: registration-time wiring may touch only `pi.on`, because the unchanged `taiknr.test.ts` Proxy throws on any other `ExtensionAPI` member (`CB-2`); the duplicate-id scenario is already satisfied by `InMemoryArchiveStore`'s `Map.set`, so dedupe would be duplicated code (`CB-4`); and `createGatedPi` plus a hand-written `session.isActive()` guard in `session-start.ts` is the complete inertness mechanism, needing no new capability (`CB-5`). `CB-1` and `CB-3` repeat the observability and map-lifecycle findings.

**Economics — `council-catalyst` (no findings).** It looked for a false compromise — a design that keeps two irreconcilable goals by weakening both — and found none. It confirms the two-phase staging/settlement split preserves intra-run model visibility and prefix caching while still stubbing aborted runs, that whole-branch hydration is Joel's accepted trade-off rather than an accidental contradiction, and that `SessionManager.inMemory` and `ExtensionRunner.emitBoundary` are exported directly, so O5 is a real choice rather than a forced one.

**What the lenses rested on.** No lens ran a check, a model call or a live TUI. Platform claims come from `Chidi` and `michael-orientation.md` in all seven reports; the critic re-read one Pi document (`compaction.md` L83–138) and the readiness lens re-read the Pi type declarations and three source ranges, and the engine's rulings re-measured the disputed paths themselves. Two unverified assumptions are worth carrying: hosted providers rejecting lone UTF-16 surrogates (`CR-3`'s premise — the ruling adopts surrogate safety regardless, since it is cheap) and Pi's own built-in output truncation, which bounds `CR-7`'s size estimate.

## Synthesis & Recommendation

**The direction: implement inside the existing modules, and let the branch be the truth about what is committed.**

Concretely, and in the order the design should argue it:

1. **One settlement pass, ordered so nothing is mutated before the drafts exist.** At `agent_before_settle`: prune ineligible items from `pendingOutputs` in place and delete their `originalContentByEntryId` keys; return `undefined` if nothing is left; call `toSettlementDrafts` **before** touching the store or draining anything; order taiknr's drafts `taiknr-side-store` first, then the `context_edit`s, all appended after `...event.entries`; then drain into the store and clear the committed keys (M4, M6). This answers `R-2` and `CR-6` without a rollback Pi's API cannot express.
2. **Eligibility stated as two checkable conditions, not as a mechanism.** A pending item commits iff `getBranch()` holds a `toolResult` message entry with that id **and** neither `getBranch()` nor `event.entries` already holds any `context_edit` targeting that id, null or non-null, from any author (M5). This is `C-1`, `CR-2`, `R-4` and `CS-3` closed by one simplification, and it keeps pre-compaction items alive by never reading `event.context.contextEntries`.
3. **Eligibility's home is `stageToolOutputs`; `toToolOutputCandidates` stays pure translation.** Join text blocks with `"\n"` when at least one is non-empty, produce `""` otherwise, and require `rawText.length > 0 && shouldArchive(...)` in the use case (M8). `shouldArchive` and its `>=` test stay untouched.
4. **The persisted item is the domain object, and the format has one definition.** A bare non-empty `ArchivedToolOutput[]` as the `taiknr-side-store` payload; a well-formed item defined once and consumed by both the write and the read side; extra unknown properties tolerated; a future breaking change gets a new `customType` rather than an envelope (M1). Batches fold in `getBranch()` order through `replaceAll`, so the last well-formed entry on the branch wins a duplicate id — no new dedupe code, which is `CB-4`'s reuse finding (M1).
5. **Everything Pi-shaped stays in the adapters and handlers.** `entryId` resolves through `event.context.contextEntries` by `toolCallId`, never by object identity and never by position except as a length-checked fallback; `turn-end.ts` keys `originalContentByEntryId` from the candidate's own `toolCallId`, so no new adapter export is needed and `Modified Capabilities` stays `None` (M7). The Pi-free store stays where it is.
6. **Two test levels, both real.** Fixtures first (`createContextStub({ branch })`, a real `InMemoryArchiveStore` in `session-start.test.ts`), layer tests next, and a zero-network session-level test driving `createAgentSession` with `fauxProvider` for the four paths that only exist at runtime (M10).
7. **No output, but named observation surfaces.** The store, the persisted `taiknr-side-store` and `context_edit` entries, `SessionConfig.state` and Pi's extension error channel — with zero `console.*` and per-handler error behaviour stated (M11).

Why this direction and not the alternatives the lenses offered: it is the only one that needs no new production file, no new dependency, no signature change and no `extension-packaging` delta, so the whole of D7's locked surface is satisfied by construction. It also keeps every correctness critical path in one place — the branch — rather than in a parallel in-memory belief, which is what `CR-1` was right to attack and wrong to fix (M4: the store is an optimistic cache that `replaceAll` reconciles; `taiknr-side-store`-first ordering is what makes a partial persist harmless).

**The cost the council accepts, stated once so the next card can act on it.** Persisting the full item duplicates every archived output's raw text in the session JSONL (`CR-7`), and whole-branch hydration keeps every item in the archive and in Jev's payload until caps land (`CS-7`, Joel's Q2). Both are recorded for `design.md` Risks and for the archive-scaling card (M3, M11) — not engineered around here.

## Michael's Rulings

`michael-council-rulings.json` holds eleven rulings, all `settled`, none marked `joel`. Each is reproduced below with what he ruled and what decided it.

**M1 — The persisted side-store payload stays a bare array, and the item shape is defined once.**
*Dispute:* readiness owner question 1 and `R-3`, `CR-10`, `CS-1`, `CB-4`, `C-5`, `CS-8` — a versioned `{ v: 1, items }` envelope or a bare `ArchivedToolOutput[]`, plus the item contract, extra-field tolerance, whole-entry or per-item skipping, and the duplicate winner.
*Ruling:* Keep the bare non-empty `ArchivedToolOutput[]` as `CustomEntry.data`. Define a well-formed item once in the design and in both deltas as a plain object with `id` (non-empty string), `toolName`, `argsSummary`, `isError` (boolean), `stub` and `rawText` (strings), tolerating extra unknown properties so additive fields such as timestamps for the archive-caps card stay compatible. A future breaking change uses a new `customType` rather than sniffing an envelope. On hydration skip any entry whose `customType` is not `TAIKNR_SIDE_STORE_TYPE`, whose `data` is not a non-empty array, or any of whose elements fails the item guard — whole entry, not per item. Fold valid batches in `getBranch()` order (root to leaf) through `replaceAll`, so on a duplicate id the last well-formed entry on the branch wins.
*Decided by:* the deletion test; D7/D9 (`TAIKNR_SIDE_STORE_TYPE` and `readArchiveBatchesFromBranch(entries): ArchivedToolOutput[][]` at `pi-archive-adapter.ts:5,25-28,53`); both spec deltas' "Data of the wrong shape". His evidence: `CustomEntry` is non-LLM internal state, `Array.isArray(entry.data)` already identifies v1, tolerating unknown properties covers additive evolution, and an incompatible future schema is skipped by v1 readers under either `{ v: 2 }` or a bumped `customType` — so the envelope fails the deletion test. `replaceAll` calls `Map.set` in iteration order over `getBranch()`, giving branch-last-wins with zero extra dedupe code.

**M2 — PoC-era sessions are ignored, with no legacy reader.**
*Dispute:* readiness owner question 2 and `R-9`.
*Ruling:* Ignore `jev-side-store` entries and `tc_N` stubs from PoC-era runs; build no legacy reader or compatibility shim, and record it as a one-line note in `design.md`'s Migration Plan. Any custom entry whose `customType` is not `taiknr-side-store` is skipped.

**M3 — Each side-store item is the full `ArchivedToolOutput`, `rawText` and `stub` included.**

**M4 — Drain at settlement, ordered safely, with the branch as the reconciler.**
*Dispute:* `CR-1` against `R-2`, `C-2`, `CS-7`, `CR-6` — retain items in `pendingOutputs` until a `context_edit` appears on `getBranch()`, or keep drain-at-settle with safe ordering.
*Ruling:* Reject `CR-1`. Keep drain-at-settlement through `commitArchivedBatch` with this order: (1) prune ineligible items from `pendingOutputs` in place, deleting their `originalContentByEntryId` keys; (2) return `undefined` if `pendingOutputs` is empty; (3) call `toSettlementDrafts` **before** mutating the store or draining `pendingOutputs`, so an unexpected throw changes nothing, and order taiknr's drafts `{ type: "custom", customType: TAIKNR_SIDE_STORE_TYPE, data: batch }` first, the `context_edit` drafts after, all appended after `...event.entries`; (4) drain `pendingOutputs` in place into the store and delete the committed ids from `originalContentByEntryId`; (5) state explicitly in `design.md` and in `specs/archive-stubbing/spec.md` that, because Pi validates and commits boundary drafts after all handlers return with no post-commit callback, `InMemoryArchiveStore` is an optimistic cache over the branch that reconciles by `replaceAll` on the next hydration event, and that side-store-first ordering is what stops a partial persist from leaving a stub without its raw record.
*Decided by:* Pi 1.5.2 mechanics (`agent-session.js:635-657,3477-3515`, `session-manager.js:974-1007`, `extensions/runner.js:798-813`); the archive-stubbing delta's "The original output is still there"; D7's `commitArchivedBatch` contract. His evidence: after a settled run the tree chain is `toolResult(X) -> assistant(final) -> settlement`, so `CR-1` would re-stub `toolResult(X)` after a `/tree` branch and hold images indefinitely; `_applyBoundaryDrafts` appends in list order, which confirms side-store-first removes the stubbed-without-archive failure mode.

**M5 — Eligibility is two checkable conditions against `getBranch()` and `event.entries`.**
*Dispute:* `C-1`, `CR-2`, `R-4`, `CS-3` — which source defines branch membership, how to fix the uncheckable "Pi's recovery omission" wording, and whether only `replacement: null` or any prior `context_edit` disqualifies.
*Ruling:* Pin the filter to two conditions and never read compaction-bounded `event.context.contextEntries`: a pending item commits iff `getBranch()` holds a `message` entry with `entry.id === id` and `entry.message.role === "toolResult"`, **and** neither `getBranch()` nor `event.entries` already holds any `context_edit` targeting that id, whether `replacement` is `null` or not. Reword the spec requirement to that author-independent rule, keeping Pi's recovery omission and a prior extension's redaction as scenarios under it.
*Decided by:* Joel's Q1 → (a) and Q2 → (a); Pi 1.5.2 `session-manager.d.ts:119` (`ContextEditEntry` carries no author field) and `session-manager.js:201-229,264` (`contextEntries` omits everything before `compaction.firstKeptEntryId`, which would wrongly drop pre-compaction items against Q2 → (a), while `getBranch()` retains the branch). Dropping on any targeting edit uses one `Set<string>` of `targetId`s, closes both null-resurrection and overwriting an earlier redaction, and enforces "Nothing commits twice" structurally.

**M6 — No signature changes; release `originalContentByEntryId` strictly and accept `argsByCallId` as a documented shortcut.**
*Dispute:* `R-1`, `CS-2`, `CB-3`, `CR-8`, `R-7` — delete `argsByCallId` keys in `turn_end` and give one settlement owner both maps, against `R-1`'s observation that the locked types allow neither.

*Decided by:* D7 (`extension-packaging` "Signatures unchanged"); `turn-end.ts:9,11` (`argsByCallId: ReadonlyMap<string, string>`) and `agent-before-settle.ts:6-11` (no `argsByCallId`); Pi 1.5.2 `types.d.ts:955` (`parentToolCallId`); the deletion test. **M7 — Zero new production files and zero new adapter exports.**
*Dispute:* `R-6`, `CR-9`, `CS-4`, zoom-out, `O4` — split the store out of the adapter, and how to pair a tool result with its entry id.

**M8 — Staging eligibility has one home, in `stageToolOutputs`.**
*Dispute:* `C-4`, `CR-5`, `R-5`, `CS-9`, `O2` — where the "no text is never staged" rule lives and how multiple text blocks join.
*Ruling:* (1) In `toToolOutputCandidates`, collect the text blocks and set `rawText` to `"\n"`-joined text when at least one block is non-empty, otherwise `""` — so image-only `[]`, `[""]` and `C-4`'s `["", ""]` all yield `""` rather than `"\n"`; (2) a whitespace-only non-empty string counts as text (`text.length > 0`, no `.trim()`); (3) `stageToolOutputs` requires `candidate.rawText.length > 0 && shouldArchive(...)`, so every caller enforces the rule from one home. `shouldArchive` is untouched and `toToolOutputCandidates` stays pure DTO translation.

**M9 — `rawText` stays verbatim; rendering is single-line and surrogate-safe.**
*Dispute:* `CR-3` and zoom-out, `O1` — surrogate-pair splitting at the 180- and 120-character cuts, and unescaped U+2028/U+2029/U+0085 in `JSON.stringify`.
*Ruling:* In `src/domain/archived-output.ts`: (1) keep `candidate.rawText` verbatim and keep `<n>` equal to `candidate.rawText.length`; (2) split on `/\r\n|\r|\n|\u2028|\u2029|\u0085/` for `<m>`, join the first three for `<preview>` with `" | "`, cut to at most 180 UTF-16 code units without splitting a surrogate pair (cut at `max - 1` when that unit is a high surrogate paired at `max`), then normalize with `.toWellFormed()`; (3) add one shared domain single-line, surrogate-safe truncation helper (120 units, the same separator class replaced by `" "`) and use it both when `tool-call.ts` summarizes `(JSON.stringify(event.input) ?? "")` and inside `buildStub` on `argsSummary` and `toolName`, so `buildStub` guarantees a well-formed single-line stub for any caller.
*Decided by:* the delta's "The stub is one line naming the output and previewing it"; a Node 26.8.1 measurement; ADR 0002's Consequences (settled stubs are permanent on the branch). His evidence: `JSON.stringify({ a: "x\u2028y\u2029z\u0085w" })` leaves those three separators unescaped, and `("a".repeat(179) + "🚀").slice(0, 180).isWellFormed()` is `false`.
